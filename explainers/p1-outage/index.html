The Incident Triage Lab is the Part 1 package: seven narrated lessons, a five-ticket assessed simulation, SCORM completion, score and resume reporting, a worksheet and a facilitator guide. The script draws on the lesson "Major Incident Management Drill". Voices are AI-generated, and so is the music (ElevenLabs Music). The outage, its clock times and its counts are an illustrative scenario; the five ticket titles and the worked answers are from the simulation.
