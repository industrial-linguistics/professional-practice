Speaker 1: What does APP 11 actually require from a SaaS provider?

Speaker 2: It demands reasonable steps to secure personal information and to destroy or de-identify it once it is no longer needed.

Speaker 1: Remember the Medibank hack—9.7 million records exposed. APP 11 "reasonable steps" failed and class actions north of $50M followed.

Speaker 2: In an SLA we convert that into encryption standards, 30-day breach notifications and secure disposal guarantees.

Speaker 1: We also need audit rights to prove those "reasonable steps" exist. Without evidence, we cannot defend ourselves to the OAIC.

Speaker 2: Include joint incident response drills so vendors are not improvising when something goes wrong.
