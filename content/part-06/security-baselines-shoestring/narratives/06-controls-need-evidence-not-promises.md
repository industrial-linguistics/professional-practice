Speaker 1: A baseline is only useful when each control has an owner, an action and evidence that it works. Start with MFA coverage and a password-vault audit, so stolen credentials and informal secret sharing are visible.

Speaker 2: Next, use a device-compliance report and a timed restore record. Encryption and backups are promises until those checks pass.

Speaker 1: Then review identity, device and cloud alerts every week, closing the actions someone actually owns.

Speaker 2: Finally, test the two-page incident guide in a tabletop and update the contact list. These six controls are a minimum working baseline, not a complete program. Revisit the owners and evidence whenever the company or its risks change.
