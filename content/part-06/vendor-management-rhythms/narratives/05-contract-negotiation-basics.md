Speaker 1: Two acronyms that get confused. An SLA commits the vendor to a level of performance. An SLR describes what they will report to you about it.

Speaker 2: Negotiate both. A vendor who performs well but can't prove it is, for governance purposes, indistinguishable from one who doesn't.

Speaker 1: Tie penalties to business impact rather than to the vendor's convenience: credits for downtime, remediation timelines, defined escalation paths.

Speaker 2: Specify the exit before you sign. Data export formats, transition assistance, knowledge transfer windows.

Speaker 1: And capture renewal notice periods and price-uplift caps in the master agreement, while you still have leverage.
