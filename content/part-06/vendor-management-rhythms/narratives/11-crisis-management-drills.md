Speaker 1: Build the crisis machinery before the crisis. A shared incident channel, an escalation ladder, an on-call rotation map that covers both organisations.

Speaker 2: Then run joint simulations. A vendor outage, a data breach, a sudden demand spike. Each one exposes different assumptions.

Speaker 1: The question that always surfaces is authority. Who can call a rollback? Who signs off on customer communications? Who decides a regulator needs notifying, and how quickly?

Speaker 2: Settle that in the drill, in daylight, rather than at two in the morning with customers watching.

Speaker 1: And capture the learnings in a post-mortem template both companies share.
