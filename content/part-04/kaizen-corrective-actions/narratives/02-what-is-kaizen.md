Speaker 1: Kaizen is continuous improvement in small increments, and the emphasis is on small.

Speaker 2: The suggestions come from the people doing the work, not from a programme office. A service desk agent tightening how they document tickets, a network admin writing a checklist for a routine task.

Speaker 1: And it lives inside daily work rather than in a separate initiative with its own meetings.

Speaker 2: Which is what makes it cultural. Over time it produces a team that expects to notice problems and expects to be listened to when they do.
