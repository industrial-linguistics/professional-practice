Speaker 1: A job title is a starting point for reading an advertisement. The useful detail is what you would produce and who would use it.

Speaker 2: Systems engineer is a good example. One employer wants someone to automate servers. Another wants someone to help customers evaluate a product.

Speaker 1: Ask about decision authority, support after release and on-call expectations. Also ask which colleagues you would depend on.

Speaker 2: In a small company, one person can hold several roles. That makes the boundaries more important to discuss. Someone still has to own the work when the other person is away.
